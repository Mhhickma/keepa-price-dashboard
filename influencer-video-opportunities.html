Qualification and data coverage

Every qualified row has an active campaign with commission ≥10%, is not apparel, has a merchant/brand/seller video and fewer than 5 observed videos, and has monthly sold ≥110% of its valid 90-day average. Missing sales history cannot pass. PPE and functional gear remain eligible.

Monthly sold is Amazon’s bracketed “bought in past month” metric, not exact units. The average uses a full 90-day time-weighted history. Video counts are Keepa-observed carousel/community counts from stored metadata unless you explicitly request a final refresh; Amazon-wide completeness is not guaranteed. Missing metadata is not zero.

Positive BSR trend means current rank is lower (better) than its historical average. It never substitutes for sales growth. Score ranks qualified rows only. Budget and slots belong to the best-commission campaign; open campaign details to compare all campaigns. Dates use UTC.
